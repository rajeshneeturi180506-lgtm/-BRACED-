Our Story

BRACED was created to make smartphone protection easier, smarter, and more reliable. We believe every smartphone deserves premium protection without complicated paperwork.

Our platform combines artificial intelligence with modern technology to simplify inspections, automate claims, and provide affordable protection plans.

Whether you own a brand-new flagship device or an older smartphone, BRACED helps keep it protected through intelligent digital services and customer-first support.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make smartphone protection simple, affordable, and powered by artificial intelligence. We strive to provide instant device inspections, transparent pricing, and fast claim processing, giving every customer complete peace of mind.

Our Vision
