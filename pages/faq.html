General Questions

What does BRACED cover?

BRACED protects your smartphone against screen damage, accidental drops, liquid damage and other covered incidents depending on your selected plan.

How do I submit a claim?

Visit the Claims page, complete the claim form, upload your device photos and submit your request for AI inspection.

How long does claim approval take?

Most claims are reviewed within 24 hours after the AI inspection is completed.

Can I protect an old smartphone?

Yes. BRACED supports many new and existing smartphones, subject to our eligibility requirements and AI inspection.

What documents are required?

You may need your purchase invoice, device details, and photos captured during the AI inspection process.

Can I cancel my protection plan?

Yes. You can cancel your protection plan according to the terms and conditions of your selected plan.
